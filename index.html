About Surjit Electricals

Surjit Electricals is a proprietorship based in Vashi, Navi Mumbai, and a Government of Maharashtra approved licensed electrical contractor and consultant.

We have worked with Mumbai and Navi Mumbai builders and developers since 1981, delivering projects from Bandra, Khar and Santacruz to Vashi, Sanpada, Uran and Thane.

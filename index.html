Experienced & Licensed Electrical Contractors

Serving builders and developers since 1981.

Surjit Electricals is a proprietorship based in Navi Mumbai, and a Government of Maharashtra approved licensed electrical contractor and consultant. Our work runs from small wiring jobs to full HT/LT substations for residential towers, hotels, malls and colleges.

Who we are

A team of qualified licensed wiremen, engineers, supervisors and foremen with decades of hands-on experience in Bandra, Khar, Santacruz, Vashi, Sanpada, Uran and Thane.

  • HT/LT cable laying teams and cable jointers

How We Work

A simple process from first call to commissioning.

  1. 1

    Enquiry & site visit

    Call or message us with your requirement. We visit the site and understand the load and layout.

  2. 2

    Planning & approvals

    We prepare the scope and handle sanctions and permissions with MSEDCL, BEST or Reliance Energy.

  3. 3

    Erection & installation

    Our licensed teams carry out substation, cabling, panel and wiring work on site.

  4. 4

    Testing & commissioning

    The installation is tested, commissioned and handed over ready to energise.
